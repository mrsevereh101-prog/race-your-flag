# Race Your Flag: the 7 launch heroes, base prompts and 4 skin prompts each

Every hero has 4 skins: Skin 1 free, Skins 2 to 4 unlock. Kapwa's Street Runner is his flag skin (Haiti); the other six heroes have a country-flag skin as Skin 4.

**No logos:** Tripo tends to put a Nike-style swoosh on sneakers. Every prompt says "plain, no logos, no brand marks, no swoosh" for shoes. If a logo still shows, say "completely plain shoes with no logo" and re-roll, or ask Claude to paint it out of the texture.

Copy each code box into Tripo (image-to-3D). Make the base hero first, then upload the base picture as a reference for that hero's 3 skins so the face and body stay the same. Heights are in each hero's heading.
